# Privacy Policy for BySismo TV

    **Last updated:** August 14, 2026

    **BySismo TV** ("Extension") does NOT collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information, browsing history, or user data.

    ### Local Storage
    The extension only uses `chrome.storage.local` to store user preferences (favorite channels, last channel, volume) locally on the
  user's device.

    ### Permissions
    * `declarativeNetRequest` & Host Permissions: Used solely to enable HLS live video streaming playback from official CDN servers.
    * `sidePanel`: Used to provide the side panel player in Google Chrome.
